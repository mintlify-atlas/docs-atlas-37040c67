---
title: "Interactive preview"
description: "Zoom, pan, and inspect your generated pattern in the canvas preview."
---

The **Vista Previa del Patrón** canvas appears on the right side of the workspace after you generate a pattern. It renders your grid in real time and updates whenever you make pencil edits.

## Canvas dimensions

The canvas has an internal resolution of **900 × 560 pixels** and scales to fill the available width of its container. The base cell size at zoom level 1 is 20 canvas pixels.

## Automatic fit on generation

Every time a new pattern is generated, the view resets to fit the entire grid within the canvas with a small margin (92% of the maximum fit size). The grid is centered both horizontally and vertically.

You can return to this fitted view at any time by clicking **Ajustar vista** in the top-right corner of the preview panel.

## Zooming

Scroll the mouse wheel over the canvas to zoom:

- **Scroll up** — zoom in by a factor of 1.12 per tick.
- **Scroll down** — zoom out by a factor of approximately 0.893 per tick.

Zoom is centered on the cursor position, so the cell under your pointer stays fixed as you scroll.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum zoom | 0.15× |
| Maximum zoom | 20× |

## Panning

Make sure **🖐 Mover** is the active tool (it is the default). Click and drag anywhere on the canvas to pan the view. The cursor changes to a grab hand while dragging.

## Symbol labels

Each cell displays its alphabetic symbol label (A, B, C, … Z, AA, AB, …) when it is large enough to read. Labels are hidden when the rendered cell is smaller than **10 canvas pixels** — this happens at low zoom levels or for large grids. Zoom in to reveal the labels.

## Transparent cells

Cells that are empty (either because they fell below the alpha threshold or were erased with the pencil) are rendered as a **checkerboard pattern** of two dark grey tones. This matches the convention used in image editors to indicate transparency.

## Switching tools

The toolbar below the panel header contains a **🖐 Mover** / **✏️ Lápiz** toggle:

- **🖐 Mover** — drag to pan the canvas. This is the default tool.
- **✏️ Lápiz** — click or drag to paint cells. When this tool is active, the cursor changes to a crosshair.

The palette color swatches only appear in the toolbar when **✏️ Lápiz** is selected. See [Editing patterns](/guides/editing-patterns) for full details on painting.
